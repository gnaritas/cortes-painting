Privacy

What this site knows about you

Very little, on purpose.

The contact form

When you send the contact form, Cortés Painting Inc receives your name, message, and any phone number or email you add — used only to answer you and quote the job. Messages are delivered by email and kept no longer than needed to follow up.

No trackers

This site loads no third-party scripts and sets no advertising or analytics cookies. The stylesheet, fonts and images are all served from this site itself. Nothing about your visit is assembled into a profile.
